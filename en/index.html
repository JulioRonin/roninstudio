02 — Cases and workflows

Lead case · Orka Oleo Group

From a spreadsheet shared every hour to a system the team uses every day.

Orka, an oils and derivatives trading company operating in Juárez and Texas, ran everything in one Excel file. Every hour, the director shared it with an outside agency that turned it into Power BI dashboards. That process was the first thing we removed. We built a platform from scratch where sales, pricing, credit and clients live in one place, with the dashboards built in.

1source of truth for sales, pricing, credit and clients

What we built
  1. 01A system from scratch: sales, clients, credit and pricing in one platform.
  2. 02Built-in dashboards that replaced the outside Power BI reports.
  3. 03AI agents for approvals, sales follow-up and reporting.
  4. 04Cross-team workshops and hands-on training for adoption.
Case · Kanri for IMC Design

From processes defined on paper to a workshop managed end to end.

IMC Design, a design and fabrication workshop in Juárez, had clear processes but none of them lived in a platform: quoting, purchasing, production and collections were tracked in sheets and messages. We built Kanri on those processes, and today the workshop runs there from quote to payment.

04 — AI agents

Agents that work inside your operation.

An agent isn't a loose chatbot. It's part of the workflow: it reads your data, does tasks with your tools and lets a person approve what matters. These two are already running.
